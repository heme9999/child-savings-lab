# October 12, 2026 calculator and status corrections

## Changes

- A shared month-end schedule now drives both contribution validation and balance simulation. The $5,000 benchmark applies to actual modeled family/employer deposits in each calendar year, not twelve times a partial-year monthly payment. Prior/outside deposits and future indexed limits are explicitly outside the check.
- The result separates the modeled contribution window cutoff, the statutory growth-period end, and the projection target. No cutoff date is invented when the projection contains no eligible month-end.
- Annual balance rows and the growth chart use the same simulation as the metrics. The final partial-year row does not introduce extra partial-month returns. New yearly deposits exclude the opening balance and optional pilot assumption. Both graph and metrics hide after edits or calculation errors.
- Eligibility now separates shared invalid input from missing account facts and missing pilot facts. Unknown citizenship or qualifying-child answers do not hide a known account path. A known disqualifying pilot fact does not demand unrelated missing answers.
- A reported existing account is distinguished from a new election. Adult-stage cases point to applicable IRA rules and trustee guidance, not repeat activation. Conflicting existing-account/SSN answers are flagged for verification rather than used to declare the account invalid.
- Proposed sources cannot carry `effectiveFrom` in the source type. Their proposed applicability, original Federal Register publication dates, linked IRS publication dates, and review dates are distinguished.
- Tool navigation identifies Trump Account scope. Articles identify the publication as publisher, without inventing a personal name, expert, or team. The editorial policy now explains AI assistance and includes a correction log.
