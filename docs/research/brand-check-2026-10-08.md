This observation is not a legal likelihood-of-confusion analysis.

This related name shares savings/laboratory wording; it prevents treating the preliminary check as an all-clear. The publication continues using its full Child Savings Lab name and does not shorten it to “Savings Lab” or claim affiliation with that credit union.
